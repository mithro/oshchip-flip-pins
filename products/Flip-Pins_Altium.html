Flip-Pins Library for Altium

The Altium library for Flip-Pins contains 3 schematic symbols, and for each symbol, you can choose between two footprints.

  • One footprint has the 3D model of just the pins, so you can visualize what your board will look like when you are finished

  • One footprint has the 3D model that includes the plastic aligner, so you can check for clearances around the aligner.
